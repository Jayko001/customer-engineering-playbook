# A future in customer service

- Customers should have access to all the dials and switches at their command. If a user contacts support, it's probably because they don't know which dial to turn, not because they can't access it
- We need to try like crazy to keep every customer. Every customer interaction is an opportunity to upsell or potentially lose a customer
- The cost of providing a single bad customer service experience has never been higher, and so the value of providing a great one has never been higher as well

> Customer retention is the new acquisition

# Humanizing the web
- The future in tech is lacking human connection. Companies actively starting to remove human elements from their advertising, it speaks to a new mindset that what matters is automation and machinery
- Removing humanity from the web is where we started to go wrong. Customers want to connect with us on a human level. They don't just want slick automations.

## How to be more human when engaging with customers
1. Go the extra mile. Take initiative to find customers info and track them down to resolve a problem
2. Reference customers personally using their name
3. Whenever possible -- offer credit or refund customers for support issues. If they complain publicly, reply publicly and seek them out privately with a reply
4. If using scripted replies, make sure they sound genuine and personal
5. Make sure the public replies, when taken out-of-context seem compassionate, helpful and friendly.

# Surprise & Delight & Reward & Celebrate
1. Crediting people's account for a month of service. Sending them a quick form email to let them know and thank them for being a customer

## What can you do to show your customers you care
1. Send handwritten notes
2. Send personal notes or emails on customers' anniversaries 
3. Offer % discount for customers after they pass the one year mark
4. Follow top customers on social media
5. Posts contests on blog and offer account credits to anyone who participates
6. Refund the last billing account when a customer has had a frustrating experience
7. Hide discounts or freebies in social media

> Customers don't expect you to be perfect. They DO expect you to fix things when they go wrong

# Finding your support voice
#example 
## Opening statements
- Hi x! Thank you for getting in touch!
- Hi x! I'd love to help you out with this.
- Hey x, Sorry to hear you're having this issue

## Closing statements
- Thanks again for contacting support
- Let us know if you need anything else
- Hope that solves all your problems. We're always here to help!

## Response for troubleshooting an issue
- Sorry to hear you're having this issue! Let me help you get this sorted out.
- Sorry about that trouble. We can definitely help you with this.
- I'm sorry you rain into that issue! If i can get some more info from you, we can get you a quick fix

## Asking more info
- We'll be happy to help troubleshoot it for you, but first we'll need some more details.
- I think i know what the issue may be, but getting some more information from you will help me know it for sure.
- It sounds look you're hitting a bug we are aware of, so I need to gather some more info from you to be sure and get that fixed for you

## Response for linking to docs
- Here is more information about that from our docs
- You can find more help for issues like this in our online knowledge base
- We've written a detailed overview of this in our help section
